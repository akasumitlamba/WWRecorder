Privacy Policy

WWRecorder is built with privacy as a core principle. Here is how we handle your data:


  • Local Processing: All video and audio processing happens locally on your machine. No media is ever uploaded to a server.
  • Offline Capability: The application does not require an internet connection to function.
  • No Tracking: We do not include telemetry, analytics, or user-tracking scripts in the application.
  • Storage: Recordings are stored in your Videos/WWRecorder folder by default. Configuration is stored in %APPDATA%.
