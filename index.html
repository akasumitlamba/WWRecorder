A complete local workflow

From capture to a share-ready file.

These are real WWRecorder screens. Follow the workflow from selecting an area to organizing and editing the result.

01

Screen recording

Record exactly the area you need.

Choose a region on any connected screen, include the cursor, and record system sound, microphone audio, or both. The floating pill keeps pause, audio, annotation and stop controls close while you work.

  • Region capture across multiple monitors
  • System sound and microphone controls
  • Pause and resume without starting a new file
02

Live and screenshot annotation

Point to the important part while it is on screen.

Draw over the live desktop during a recording, or open a screenshot in the image editor. Use pencil, highlighter, arrows, shapes and text, with undo, redo and eraser controls.

  • Live annotations appear in the recording
  • Screenshot editor with crop, rotate and flip
  • Editable text, colours and line thickness
03

Recent Files

Open, edit or move a capture without searching for it.

The sidebar shows recent recordings and screenshots with previews. Drag a file directly into another app, or use the vertical ⋮ menu for the available file actions.

  • Videos: Play, Edit, Copy, Rename and Delete
  • Photos: Open & Edit, Copy, Rename and Delete
  • Search and open the capture folder in Explorer
04

Video player and editor

Clean up the result without a production suite.

Review the recording, save a frame, or open Edit for the changes people need most before sharing.

  • Trim the beginning or end
  • Remove unwanted sections
  • Mute exported audio or add timed text

A focused Windows tool

Useful where it matters.
Deliberately uncomplicated.

WWRecorder is designed for people who need to capture and explain something quickly—not people building a multi-track film project.

GOOD FIT

Choose WWRecorder when you need to…

  • Record a lesson, walkthrough, demo, or bug report
  • Capture system sound, microphone audio, or both
  • Draw attention to something while recording
  • Trim mistakes and remove a few unwanted sections
NOT TRYING TO BE

A full video-production suite.

  • No multi-track editing or advanced color grading
  • No streaming studio or scene-management system
  • No account, subscription, or cloud workspace
  • No forced upload before you can use your own files

Under the hood

Engineered for
reliable capture.

Pixels, audio, timing, and temporary media each have a clear owner. That matters when the computer is busy, an audio device changes, or final processing fails.

Open the engineering deep dive →
01 / TIMINGFrame timing follows real time

Missed scheduler slots are counted instead of filled with stale frames, then the final video is normalized to a stable 30 FPS.

wall clock → setpts → CFR 30
02 / AUDIOAudio stays on Windows endpoints

System sound and microphone capture use short WASAPI reads, follow default-device changes, and mix locally.

WASAPI · 48 kHz · Opus
03 / RECOVERYRecovery comes before cleanup

Work media is removed only after a non-empty final MKV is proven, so a processing failure does not erase the only copy.

saved · discarded · recoverable
