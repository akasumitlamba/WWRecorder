02 / Video timing

Video and audio stay together.

Recordings use a 30 FPS timeline. If capture slows, the previous screen image is held until the next frame is available.

Simplified recording flow
Capture the selected area
Keep screen and sound on one timeline
Hold the previous image during capture delays

Capture resumes with the newest available screen image.

Simplified save flow
Remove paused intervals
Combine video and selected audio
Save an H.264 / Opus MKV file

Paused time is left out of the saved recording.
