1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how WWRecorder (the “App”), developed and published by Sumit Kumar Lamba (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you install and use the App on a Windows device.

This policy covers the WWRecorder desktop application. Third-party websites you choose to visit, including GitHub, are governed by their own privacy policies.

2. Data the App handles

WWRecorder handles the following information only to provide features you choose to use:

Screen and media content

  • Pixels from the screen region you select for a recording or screenshot.
  • System audio when system-audio capture is enabled.
  • Microphone audio when microphone capture is enabled.
  • Text, drawings, crop selections, and other edits you add to a capture.

This content can include personal or sensitive information visible or audible in the area you choose to capture. The App processes it locally to create the media file you requested.

Settings and file information

The App stores preferences such as your output folder, configured keyboard shortcuts, audio defaults, appearance settings, update preferences, and dock position. It also reads basic file information from your selected output folder to show recent captures.

Global keyboard shortcuts

WWRecorder listens for your configured global shortcuts so recording and screenshot actions work while other apps are active. The App does not create a history of your keystrokes and does not transmit them.

Clipboard content

When automatic clipboard copy is enabled, WWRecorder places a newly saved capture or its file reference on the Windows clipboard. The App does not upload clipboard content.

What WWRecorder does not collect We do not collect names, email addresses, account credentials, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, payment information, browsing history, or usage analytics through the App.

3. Local storage and diagnostics

WWRecorder creates and uses files on your device:

  • Recordings, screenshots, and exports are saved in the output folder you select.
  • Preferences are stored in %APPDATA%\WWRecorder\config.json.
  • Local application and crash logs are stored under %APPDATA%\WWRecorder.
  • Temporary recording files may be kept in the Windows temporary folder to finish processing or recover media after a failure.
  • If launch-at-startup is enabled, WWRecorder adds an entry to the current user’s Windows startup registry location.

Logs are stored locally and are not sent to us automatically. They may contain technical error details and local file paths, but they are not designed to contain captured screen or audio content. If you voluntarily share a log when requesting support, we will use it only to investigate your request.

4. Network activity and third parties

On launch and when you manually check for updates, WWRecorder requests the latest release information from the GitHub Releases API. If you choose to download an available update, the installer is downloaded from a GitHub release address. These requests use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Like any internet request, GitHub may receive technical connection information such as your IP address, request time, and the App’s update-checker user-agent string. GitHub processes that information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive a user-level update history from these requests.

Other than update checks, update downloads you request, and links you choose to open, WWRecorder does not upload your recordings, screenshots, audio, settings, or logs to us or to a cloud service.

5. Sharing, selling, and advertising

We do not sell or rent personal information. The App contains no advertising SDKs and does not share captured content with data brokers, advertisers, or analytics providers.

Your content leaves your device only when you choose to move, copy, upload, share, or open it with another application or service. That recipient’s terms and privacy practices then apply.

6. Retention, control, and deletion

Because your recordings, screenshots, exports, settings, and logs are stored locally, you control how long they remain on your device.

  • Delete media using WWRecorder or Windows File Explorer.
  • Clear clipboard content by copying something else or using Windows clipboard controls.
  • Reset settings in the App or remove the %APPDATA%\WWRecorder folder after closing the App.
  • During uninstall, choose the option to remove WWRecorder’s local configuration data when offered.

Successfully processed temporary recording files are removed automatically. Recovery media left after an interrupted or failed recording may remain in the Windows temporary folder for up to seven days so that the only playable copy is not silently lost.

We normally hold no App account or analytics record that can be accessed, corrected, or deleted on our servers because the App does not create accounts or send such data to us. Information you voluntarily submit through GitHub support channels is retained and controlled according to GitHub’s features and policies.

7. Security

WWRecorder minimizes remote data handling by processing capture content locally and using HTTPS for update-related network requests. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for choosing what appears in a capture, protecting your Windows account and device, and sharing exported files only with intended recipients.

8. Children’s privacy

WWRecorder is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. Because the App records user-selected screen and audio content locally, a parent or guardian should supervise a child’s use and any sharing of resulting files.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features or legal obligations change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will show when revisions were made. Material changes will be published at this same URL.

10. Contact

If you have a privacy question or believe WWRecorder is not behaving as described here, contact the developer through the project’s GitHub issue tracker. Please do not attach a private recording, screenshot, log, or other sensitive file to a public issue.
